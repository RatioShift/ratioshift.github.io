How to follow this roadmap

The sequence is a flexible learning map, not a strict university syllabus. Finish the prerequisite topics shown on each node before moving forward. Week estimates assume roughly 8 focused study hours per week; adjust them to your own pace and course requirements.

Foundations first

Build algebra, functions, trigonometry, logic and proof skills before moving into calculus, linear algebra, probability and algorithms.

Career paths are selective

AI/ML, Data Science, Competitive Programming, Systems, Security, Graphics and Theory highlight different topic subsets and shared prerequisites.

Learn by doing

For every topic, solve problems, write short proofs or implement a small experiment. Reading alone is not enough to build practical fluency.

Optional means optional

Real analysis, abstract algebra, measure-theoretic probability and formal proofs are valuable depth branches, but most CS learners can postpone them until a goal requires them.
